Website Design UI

A clean and conversion-focused website UI for a digital-first brand.

Project
Website Design UI
Timeline
6 weeks
Tools
Figma, FigJam, Notion
1. The Problem

The old site had high bounce rates and weak information hierarchy. Visitors were not understanding the offer quickly enough.

Target user: First-time visitors evaluating the service in under two minutes.

This mattered because unclear messaging directly reduced demo bookings and conversion quality.

2. Research & Discovery
  • Surveyed 32 users on trust factors and key decision triggers.
  • Ran a competitive teardown of 8 direct competitors.
  • Desk research revealed best-performing patterns in hero copy and CTA placement.

Key insights: users wanted social proof above the fold, fewer jargon-heavy sections, and a clearer step-by-step flow to contact.

3. Define

Persona 1: Startup founder, time-poor, compares multiple vendors quickly.

Persona 2: Operations lead, detail-oriented, needs credibility and process clarity.

HMW: How might we help users understand value and act confidently within 90 seconds?

Goals: improve clarity, raise trust signals, and increase conversion intent.

4. Ideation

Explored three concepts: narrative-first, feature-first, and proof-first layouts using quick wire sketches and sticky-note mapping.

I chose the proof-first direction because testing showed users trusted concrete outcomes and client evidence before feature details.

5. Design Process
  • Built low-fidelity wireframes for homepage and pricing.
  • Moved into high-fidelity with responsive breakpoints for desktop and mobile.
  • Moodboard direction: minimal editorial with strong contrast and calm neutral surfaces.
  • Typography: Inter for readability and system familiarity.
  • Colors: neutral base + blue accent to guide CTA hierarchy and trust.
