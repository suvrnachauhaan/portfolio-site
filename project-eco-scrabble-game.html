Eco Scrabble Game

An educational word game that turns sustainability concepts into playful challenges.

1. Cover / Hero
Project
Eco Scrabble Game
Role
UX Designer + Game Flow Design
Tools
Figma, Miro, Google Sheets

Timeline: 7 weeks

One-line description: Designed a multiplayer word game where eco-themed mechanics improve learning and replay value.

2. The Problem

Most educational games feel either too textbook-like or too game-heavy to teach meaningfully.

Target user: teens and young adults interested in climate topics but not formal courses.

The challenge was balancing fun with learning outcomes without making either side feel forced.

3. Research & Discovery
  • Surveyed 25 students about what makes educational games enjoyable.
  • Benchmarked 5 word games and 4 learning apps.
  • Analyzed where users dropped off during tutorial-heavy game onboarding.

Key insights: users preferred "learn by play," short rounds, and feedback tied to meaning, not just points.

4. Define

Persona 1: Competitive player who wants quick rounds and leaderboard progression.

Persona 2: Purpose-driven learner who wants short educational takeaways.

HMW: How might we make sustainability learning feel naturally embedded in gameplay?

Goals: simple onboarding, fast rounds, meaningful eco-context per word play.

5. Ideation

Explored three mechanics: bonus tiles, challenge cards, and timed eco-quests. Chose challenge cards because they gave educational depth without slowing the core loop.

Mind maps helped connect vocabulary domains (waste, water, energy, biodiversity) to scoring rules.

6. Design Process
  • Progressed from paper board sketches to low-fi wireframes and then high-fi game HUD screens.
  • Moodboard: playful but clean, inspired by board-game tactility.
  • Typography: readable sans-serif at game scale with strong score legibility.
  • Color decisions: green/blue cues for positive eco-actions and amber for penalties.
7. Final Designs
Game Lobby
Sets match mode, player count, and quick rule hints before entering the round.
Main Board
Primary gameplay surface with score states and eco challenge card prompts.
Word Validation
Confirms accepted words and gives educational context on selected eco terms.
Results + Learnings
End screen combines rank outcome with short sustainability facts from the round.
