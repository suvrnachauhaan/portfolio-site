Eco Scrabble Game

An educational word game that turns sustainability concepts into playful challenges.

Project
Eco Scrabble Game
Timeline
7 weeks
Tools
Figma, Miro, Google Sheets
1. The Problem

Residents in housing societies often lack awareness of how their daily actions affect sustainability systems such as waste, water, and energy management.

2. Research & Discovery

Conducted primary research with the society manager and mapped the functioning of the housing corporation through a system map to understand stakeholders, processes, and sustainability touchpoints.

3. Define

Identified the need for a simple and engaging educational intervention that could help residents better understand sustainability in a relatable way.

4. Ideation & Design Process

Explored familiar interaction formats and chose Scrabble as a base concept. Reimagined it into Eco Scrabble, a game that teaches sustainability concepts through play.

5. Final Outcome

Designed Eco Scrabble, a fun and interactive board game that promotes sustainability awareness within housing societies by combining learning with a familiar gameplay experience.
