What I Believe
Design is not decoration. It's structured problem-solving with empathy. Aesthetics attract — functionality sustains. Logic and creativity aren't opposites. They're collaborators.
How I Think
CS trained me to think in systems — inputs, constraints, flows, outcomes. I ask "why" before "what." I prototype to think. I question patterns that exist just because they always have.
On AI
Not a replacement — an amplifier. Used well, it frees designers to focus on judgment, ethics, and the things machines can't replicate.
