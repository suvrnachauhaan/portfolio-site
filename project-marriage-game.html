Marriage Game

A social game concept designed to spark conversation, humor, and shared decision-making.

Project
Marriage Game
Timeline
4 weeks
Tools
Figma, Notion, Protopie
1. The Problem

Many social games are either repetitive after one session or hard to onboard in group settings.

Target user: couples and friend groups who want engaging, light-weight social gameplay.

The game needed to be easy to learn in under 2 minutes and fun enough to replay.

2. Research & Discovery
  • Interviewed 10 users who regularly play social and party games.
  • Compared interaction flow in 7 party apps and card-based games.
  • Reviewed replayability triggers: surprise prompts, short rounds, and role switching.

Key insights: people enjoyed playful disagreement moments but disliked long setup and rule-heavy instructions.

3. Define

Persona 1: Casual social host wanting low-friction game setup.

Persona 2: Couple looking for an interactive date-night activity.

HMW: How might we design a social game that creates laughter and conversation in short loops?

Goals: quick start, strong replayability, and clear result moments.

4. Ideation

Explored voting cards, reaction timers, and challenge modes. Chose "prompt + prediction" because it generated natural tension and conversation with minimal complexity.

Early concepts tested whether scoring should be competitive or cooperative; hybrid scoring performed best.

5. Design Process
  • Mapped complete game loop from lobby to final compatibility reveal.
  • Low-fi flow focused on one-thumb interaction and fast turn transitions.
  • Moodboard: playful, celebratory, but not childish.
  • Typography and color: bold headings for prompts, warm accent palette for emotional cues.
6. Final Designs
Start Screen
Allows players to select mode, participants, and quick tutorial without friction.
Prompt Round
Displays concise prompts and time-limited decisions to keep pacing energetic.
Reveal Moment
Shows where predictions matched or diverged, triggering discussion and humor.
Compatibility Summary
Summarizes round patterns and suggests rematch prompts for replayability.
