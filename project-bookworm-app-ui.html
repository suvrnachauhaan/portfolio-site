Bookworm App UI

A reading app focused on easy discovery, daily habits, and smarter progress tracking.

1. Cover / Hero
Project
Bookworm App UI
Role
UI/UX Designer
Tools
Figma, Maze, Illustrator

Timeline: 5 weeks

One-line description: Designed a mobile reading app that helps users discover books and sustain reading momentum.

2. The Problem

Readers downloaded book apps but abandoned them because recommendation quality felt generic and progress tracking felt boring.

Target user: casual readers who want guidance without complexity.

This mattered because retention in week 1 was the core business bottleneck.

3. Research & Discovery
  • Collected responses from 40 readers via online survey.
  • Compared 6 reading apps on onboarding and recommendations.
  • Desk research on habit loops and streak-based engagement mechanics.

Key insights: people want curated lists by mood, fast sample previews, and progress feedback that feels rewarding but not childish.

4. Define

Persona 1: Commuter reader, reads 20 minutes daily on mobile.

Persona 2: Weekend binge reader who needs discovery prompts.

HMW: How might we help readers quickly find books they love and keep reading consistently?

Goals: reduce onboarding time, improve discovery quality, and increase week-1 retention.

5. Ideation

Explored list-based navigation, card-swiping recommendations, and a shelf metaphor. Chose the shelf + card hybrid because users could browse fast without losing context.

Early paper sketches helped validate information density before polished UI exploration.

6. Design Process
  • Low-fi wireframes for onboarding, home, details, and reading session.
  • High-fi pass added visual hierarchy and clear micro-interactions.
  • Moodboard direction: warm editorial with soft depth and tactile cards.
  • Typography: Inter for body and DM Serif accents for book personality.
  • Color decisions: muted neutrals + teal highlights to cue actions and progress.
7. Final Designs
Onboarding
Collects genre preferences in under a minute to personalize the first feed.
Home Feed
Shows mood-based rails and resume reading actions at top priority.
Book Detail
Highlights summary, reviews, and sample-read CTA with low-friction decision points.
Progress
Visualizes daily streak and chapter completion to reinforce habit continuity.
